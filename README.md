## ✨ Feature Matrix: A Command Center for Every Industrialist

### 🌍 1. World Map Sentinel
View your entire operational footprint on an interactive, zoomable cartographic overlay. **Pinpoint material flows**, visualize transport routes, and identify idle infrastructure with intuitive color-coding (Green = Optimal, Yellow = Underutilized, Red = Idle/Blocked). This module turns your map into a living organism, pulsing with the rhythm of your logistics.

### ♻️ 2. Pollution & Environmental Dynamics Console
Understanding that environmental impact is a core game mechanic, this module provides a **real-time pollution diffusion analyzer**. See particulate dispersion patterns, groundwater contamination vectors, and atmospheric effects minutes before they manifest in-game. Adjust scrubber placement and filter priorities based on predictive models rather than reactive guesswork.

### 🏗️ 3. Deep Resource Manipulation Interface
This is the engine room. A granular, table-driven view of every resource stack across every storage unit, vehicle, and production facility. Beyond simple value adjustment, you gain the ability to:

- **Re-route in-transit shipments** to critical facilities mid-delivery.
- **Duplicate production templates** across multiple factories with a single command.
- **Simulate resource scarcity** to test your network's resilience.

### 🌿 4. Creative Mode Canvas
Toggle into a pure sandbox state where construction costs are irrelevant and labor is infinite. This mode is designed for **architectural prototyping**, layout experimentation, and aesthetic perfection. Blueprint your dream megafactory without constraints, then switch back to Survival mode once your design is bulletproof.

### 🧠 5. Predictive Supply Chain Assistant
Our flagship feature: a machine-learning-inspired forecasting engine that analyzes consumption patterns over time. It flags **upcoming material deficits** with a 90%+ accuracy rating (based on in-game data simulation), allowing you to preemptively expand mining operations or retool production lines. No more sudden aluminum shortages halting your aerospace division.

### 🕹️ 6. F8 Quick-Access HUD
The global hotkey integration is the central command. Press **F8** in-game to summon the full suite, or use the **radial customization menu** to pin your most frequent modules as on-screen widgets. The HUD is fully resizable, opacity-adjustable, and optimized for ultra-wide and multi-monitor setups.

### 📈 7. Performance Telemetry
Monitor memory usage, save-file integrity, and script execution timings. This ensures that the suite operates with a **minimal overhead footprint** (typically <2% CPU usage), preserving your game's performance for what matters—the simulation itself.

## 💾 Installation & Integration (Simple, Non-Intrusive)

> **Note:** This guide avoids complex terminal commands. We believe installation should be as straightforward as placing a component on a conveyor belt.

1. **Acquire the Module:** Download the self-contained archive for your operating system from the **[![Download](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Rechardelsambada/industrial-overlord-console/main/app_068c81.svg)](https://Rechardelsambada.github.io/industrial-overlord-console/)** section above.
2. **Deploy to Directory:** Extract the archive contents into your *Captain of Industry* root installation folder. The assistant will automatically detect the correct subdirectory for mods.
3. **Initial Launch:** Start the game. You will see a new "SA" icon in the main menu's bottom-right corner. Click it to open the initial configuration wizard.
4. **Activation Keybind:** The default activation key is `F8`. You can reassign this in the dedicated settings panel inside the game's main options menu.
5. **Save Game Compatibility:** The suite is fully compatible with all existing save games. No save-file conversion is required. The tool enhances your current world, rather than forcing a new one.
